Open the portal by name

You reached this server as {{host}}, but because of the way it interfaces to the KVMs, the portal only works through a tunnel and at {{portalUrl}}.

Learn more

Each KVM gets its own hostname, such as {{exampleKvm}}, so that its web interface and the same-site cookies from it work unmodified inside the portal. Your browser has to reach the portal {{portalUrl}} for that to work.

From outside the lab

Forward a local port to the portal over SSH:

ssh -L {{port}}:localhost:{{port}} {{serverName}}

Then open {{portalUrl}}. Chrome and Firefox send every *.localhost name to your own machine, so this needs no DNS or hosts file changes. Any free local port works in place of the first {{port}}.
